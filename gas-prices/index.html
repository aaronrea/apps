    How this decides

    Sorted cheapest first. Costco is the benchmark every price is measured against; Wawa is the default and wins unless it drifts more than 5% over Costco — then the detour is worth it. Anything more than 5% over Costco is flagged red.

    Unconfirmed: the 5% rule is applied to Wawa too — preferred, but not exempt. Under the line the answer is always Wawa; over it, the Costco run. Say the word if you meant Wawa to be exempt, or wanted a cents-per-gallon cutoff instead of a percentage.
