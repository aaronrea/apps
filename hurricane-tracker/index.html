      How this decides

      NHC publishes every active system worldwide and the Atlantic outlook for disturbances that don't have a full advisory yet. This page keeps only what could plausibly reach Tampa/the Gulf Coast, Texas, Florida, the Bahamas, or Honduras/the western Caribbean — a bounding box for storms with a known position, plus a keyword match on the outlook text for invests that don't have one yet. A GitHub Action re-fetches both every 3 hours, matching NHC's own advisory and outlook clock (more often near landfall, when NHC itself switches to 3-hourly intermediate advisories).
